# Provider source policy

The public index contains real organizations only. A record may be added after directly opening the organization's own website and recording the exact page URL and review date. Website text may be summarized in Armenian, Russian, and English, but the summary must stay within what the source actually says. A city, service, quality standard, accreditation, response promise, or manufacturing capability is omitted if the source does not support it. No fictional example belongs in `providers.js`.

Each record needs a stable ID, three language display names and summaries, sector, city, service terms, `sourceUrl`, and `sourceChecked` in `YYYY-MM-DD` form. Source URLs use HTTPS. List service descriptions as provider statements, not platform endorsements. A profile link points to the official source. If a source changes or disappears, remove or review the affected claim before republishing.

The previous demonstration's six invented companies have been deleted from product data. Automated browser checks inject separate fixture records and never publish them. Counts and map markers are computed from the product records; they are not estimates of Armenia's whole life sciences market.

To research a candidate, record:

| Field | What to capture |
| --- | --- |
| Organization | Name on its own website and official URL |
| Capability | Exact page and wording that supports each listed service |
| Location | Address or city from the organization's website |
| Review | UTC date the page was opened and checked |
| Translation | Editorial summary in Armenian, Russian, and English, without adding claims |
